How scoring works

  • 50 / 80 / 150 for a drone, weevil or rootkit patched while it is sitting in the formation.
  • 100 / 160 / 400 for the same three caught mid-dive. Waiting for them to come to you is worth roughly double.
  • 1000 for shooting down the rootkit that forked your duck, while it is diving. You get the duck back and it flies alongside you, firing double. Shoot that rootkit while it is parked in the formation and the duck goes with it.
  • 200 a wave, times the wave number, for clearing every bug.
  • 120 a bug on a regression sweep, plus 3000 for letting none of them past. Every fourth wave is a sweep, and nothing shoots back.
  • Three ducks, an extra at 20,000 and every 60,000 after that. The run ends when the last one goes.

Every run posted here is replayed on the server from its seed and its keystrokes before it is counted, so a score is something the server works out rather than something the browser claims.
